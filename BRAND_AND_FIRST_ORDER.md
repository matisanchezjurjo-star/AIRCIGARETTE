# Brand Name + First Order

## Naming rules
- **Stay away from "Air" / "Fume" / "Fumo".** AirFume already holds that space in Argentina, and a similar name risks an INPI opposition and makes us look like a copy.
- Choose a name that is short, easy to say in rioplatense Spanish, and works as an Instagram handle and a `.com.ar` domain.
- The name should sound like a **ritual, a pause, a calm moment**, not like smoking. That keeps us away from ANMAT and tobacco-advertising problems.
- It should look good **laser-engraved on wood** (the supplier needs a vector logo).

## Shortlist

| Name | Why it works | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **TILO** ⭐ | "Tomate un tilo" is the Argentine idiom for "calm down". Linden (tilo) is also a wood. Four letters, it engraves well, and it suggests calm. | Common word: register it as a word + logo mark. Check for herbal-tea brands in the same class. |
| **RESPIRO** | "Tomate un respiro": a pause, a breather. Positive, not about smoking. | Long-ish. Possible conflicts in wellness brands. |
| **CEIBO** | Argentina's national tree and flower. Local, premium feel, good for a wood product. | Less obvious what the product does; the name relies on branding. |
| **SOPLO** | A breath of air, light and playful. | Could read as "blow" rather than "inhale". |
| **RITO** | Fits the ritual positioning. | AirFume already says "Todo un ritual", so it looks close in marketing. |
| **PITÁ** | Very Argentine ("pitar" = to take a puff), memorable, works for young audiences. | It evokes smoking directly, so it carries the most regulatory and advertising risk. |

**Recommendation: TILO**, for example "TILO — tomate un tilo", "Tu pausa con sabor", "Aire con sabor, sin humo". Flavor packs could be "TILO Menta", "TILO Sandía", etc.

### Verify before you commit to a name (about 1 hour)
1. INPI trademark search (portaltramites.inpi.gob.ar), mainly classes 34, 3, 5 and 35.
2. Instagram / TikTok handle (`tilo.ar`, `somostilo`, `tilo.arg`…).
3. Domain on nic.ar (`tilo.com.ar`).
4. A Google search for "tilo" in vapes, wellness and aromas in Argentina.
