# AIRCIGARETTE — Project Brief

E-commerce business that **imports flavored-air inhalers ("air cigarettes") from China** and sells them in **Argentina**.

## The product

- A reusable inhaler rod (wood body + aluminum/steel mouthpiece) with a replaceable **flavored cotton core**.
- No smoke, no vapor, no nicotine, no battery. The user draws flavored air through it.
- Sold as a device plus refill packs of flavors (mint, lemon, grapefruit, maple pepper, cinnamon, raspberry, blueberry, cranberry, vanilla, orange, strawberry, coffee…).
- Positioning: an oral-fixation and habit replacement for smokers and vapers ("cambiá tus hábitos").

Ask the supplier before ordering:
- What exactly the unit price covers: the device, one core, or a flavor pack.
- Refill core price, flavor count per pack, and how long a core lasts.
- Custom branding (laser-engraved logo, custom pouches and box) and its MOQ.
- Flavor ingredient sheet / MSDS (needed for any Argentine regulatory question).
- Shipping options: air vs. sea, DDP vs. FOB.

## Reference competitor: AirFume (@airfumearg)

- About 5k Instagram followers, verified, site `airfume.com.ar`, sales over WhatsApp.
- Looks like the same kind of device, rebranded: wood plus metal, premium gift box ("Bienvenido al lado del buen hábito"), custom flavor pouches (Sandía, Menta…).
- Their marketing:
  - Lifestyle reels, UGC and testimonials ("Calma mi ansiedad", "Ahora llega a los 90 minutos sin ahogarse").
  - Answers the "is it a vape?" question head-on ("¿Parece un vap? Mirá de cerca").
  - Seasonal campaigns (Halloween / Sandía).
  - Pitches the money saved against buying cigarettes.
- Takeaway: the market is already validated in Argentina. We have to stand out on price, branding, flavors or distribution, and we must not copy their brand or creatives.

## Key risks to check early

1. **ANMAT:** Disposición 3226/2011 bans e-cigarettes. This device is not electronic and has no nicotine, but:
   - Health claims such as "dejar de fumar" or "calma la ansiedad" can get it treated as a health/medical product.
   - Keep the messaging on "aire saborizado / hábito / ritual".
   - Get a professional opinion on how the flavor cores are classified.
2. **Tobacco advertising law (Ley 26.687):** avoid ads that promote or look like smoking.
3. **Import:** decide between the courier regime (small, fast batches) and a formal import with a registered importer (CUIT + Aduana). Check the current limits and taxes.
4. **Trademark:** register our own brand with INPI before investing in branding.
5. **Margins:** landed cost = FOB + freight + duties/taxes + packaging + fulfillment + payment fees + ad cost per sale.

## Proposed roadmap

1. Choose a brand name and identity.
2. Order samples from 2–3 suppliers and compare quality and flavor intensity.
3. Build a landed-cost and pricing model (USD → ARS).
4. Set up the store. Tiendanube is the local standard, with Mercado Pago and Andreani/OCA/Correo shipping. Add a WhatsApp catalog.
5. Content plan: Instagram/TikTok reels, UGC, flavor-drop campaigns, and a subscription for refills.
6. Launch with a small first batch, measure CAC and repeat-purchase rate, then scale the order to the ≥500 tier.
